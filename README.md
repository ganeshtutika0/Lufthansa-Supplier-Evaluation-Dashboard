# Flight Simulator Supplier Evaluation – Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Analysis

## 1. Project Title

**Flight Simulator Supplier Evaluation – Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Analysis**

---

## 2. Short Description / Purpose

This project presents a Power BI dashboard designed to evaluate and compare the Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) of Full Flight Simulators from different suppliers.

The dashboard follows the analytical structure and business context of TCO and supplier evaluation activities performed during my internship at **Lufthansa Aviation Training**, within the **Strategic Purchasing and Data Analysis** area.

The analysis focuses on understanding major lifecycle cost components, evaluating supplier differences, analyzing downtime impact, and supporting data-driven procurement decisions.

To respect confidentiality requirements, the GitHub version uses **dummy/synthetic data** created solely for demonstration purposes. No confidential Lufthansa Aviation Training data is included.

---

## 5. Features & Highlights

### Business Problem

Supplier evaluation for full flight simulators involves multiple cost factors that need to be considered throughout the simulator lifecycle. Looking only at the initial purchase price does not provide a complete picture of the overall cost of ownership.

Downtime can introduce additional costs, while differences in utilization can also affect the operational cost perspective.

---

### Goal of the Dashboard

The goal of this dashboard is to:

- Provide an end-to-end view of Total Cost of Ownership.
- Compare TCO between simulator suppliers.
- Analyze major lifecycle cost components.
- Evaluate the financial impact of simulator downtime.
- Compare supplier utilization.
- Calculate simulator training cost per hour.
- Perform sensitivity analysis using adjustable downtime assumptions.
- Support data-driven supplier evaluation and procurement analysis.

---

### TCO Components

The dashboard evaluates:

- **CAPEX**
- **Maintenance Cost**
- **Operation Cost**
- **Upgrade Cost**
- **Downtime Cost**

These components are combined to provide a broader view of simulator lifecycle costs.

---

### Walkthrough of Key Visuals

- **KPI Cards:** TCO, purchase cost, downtime, utilization, adjusted TCO, and cost per hour.
- **Supplier Comparison:** Direct comparison of TCO and cost components between suppliers.
- **Lifecycle Trend:** Year-by-year analysis of downtime-related costs.
- **Utilization Analysis:** Comparison of simulator utilization between suppliers.
- **Sensitivity Analysis:** Interactive adjustment of downtime assumptions and its impact on adjusted TCO.

---

### Business Impact & Insights

#### The Problem

Supplier evaluation for full flight simulators involves multiple cost factors that need to be considered over the simulator lifecycle. Looking only at the initial purchase price does not provide a complete picture of the potential cost of ownership.

Furthermore, downtime can introduce additional costs, while differences in utilization can affect the operational cost perspective.

#### The Solution

To address this challenge, I developed an interactive **Power BI TCO dashboard** that consolidates the major lifecycle cost components into a single analytical view.

The dashboard enables users to:

- Compare supplier TCO using a consistent analytical structure.
- Break down overall TCO into individual cost components.
- Analyze the financial impact of downtime.
- Compare supplier utilization rates.
- Evaluate simulator training cost per hour.
- Adjust downtime assumptions through an interactive sensitivity parameter.
- Observe the resulting impact on adjusted TCO.

#### Value of the Solution

By bringing these elements together in one interactive dashboard, the solution provides a structured way to analyze supplier differences and understand the factors driving simulator lifecycle costs.

The approach demonstrates how **Power BI, DAX, and interactive data visualization** can transform TCO data into a management-oriented analytical tool for **strategic purchasing and supplier evaluation**.
